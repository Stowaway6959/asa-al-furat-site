How to Get Her Live on Each Ordering Platform
Uber Eats restaurants.ubereats.com
1
Go to restaurants.ubereats.com → click "Get Started." Enter restaurant name, address, phone, email.
2
Upload menu — items, descriptions, photos, prices. Use the same items from the website. Photos make a big difference.
3
Bank info + tax ID — EIN or SSN for payouts. Direct deposit to her bank. Required before going live.
4
Tablet or phone app — Uber Eats sends a free tablet for orders. Or use the app on her existing phone.
5
Goes live in 3–5 business days after submission + approval. Commission: 15–30% per order.
Square integration: If she's on Square POS, Uber Eats can sync directly — menu updates automatically, no double entry.
Grubhub get.grubhub.com/restaurant
1
get.grubhub.com/restaurant → "Partner with us." Fill out restaurant info — name, address, hours, cuisine type.
2
A Grubhub rep calls — usually within 1–2 business days. They walk through the contract. Commission is negotiable for small independents — push for 10–15%.
3
Menu build — their team can build it from photos you send them. Faster than doing it yourself.
4
Bank + EIN — same as Uber Eats. Weekly ACH payout to her bank account.
5
Goes live in 5–7 business days. Free tablet included for order management.
Tip: Grubhub has a smaller user base in mid-size cities but zero incremental work once live. Worth adding — any order it sends is pure upside.
Square Online — Direct Ordering squareup.com/online-store
Why this matters: 0% commission. Every order she takes through her own site keeps 15–30% more than DoorDash. Pays for the website in a few orders.
1
Log into Square Dashboard → Online → Get Started (or "Online Store" in left nav). Free plan is enough to start.
2
Items auto-import from her existing Square POS. She doesn't have to re-enter the menu — it's already there.
3
Enable "Pickup" or "Delivery" — set pickup window times. Delivery radius optional (uses her own driver or no delivery).
4
Copy the ordering link from the Square dashboard — something like squareup.com/store/asa-al-furat. Paste that URL into the website's "Order Direct" button.
5
Orders flow into her existing Square POS — same screen she already uses. No extra hardware or training.
