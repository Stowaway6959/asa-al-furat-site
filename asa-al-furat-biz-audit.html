The Story — Why This Is Your Best Pitch
🇮🇶 Background — Know This Before You Call

Imtihan Alaid was 11 years old when ISIS invaded Mosul. Her family fled their home in Iraq for Turkey — taking only what they could carry. In Turkey and later in Springfield's Ozarks community, her mother's daily cup of tea with cardamom was the constant that felt like home.

After being laid off in January 2025, Imtihan started selling traditional Iraqi desserts at the C-Street City Market. The response was overwhelming — Springfield had never tasted anything like it. Within months she had enough confidence and capital to sign a lease on 1,300 square feet in the Holland Building, the same space that housed the European Cafe.

"My goal is to share my culture, and I want people to know Iraq in a good way."
— Imtihan Alaid

Asa Al-Furat (عصا الفرات) opened September 8, 2025. It is the only authentic Iraqi bakery and coffee shop in Springfield, possibly in all of the Ozarks. Everything is handmade fresh daily — baklava, kunafa, kleicha, znoud el sit — recipes carried out of Iraq.

UPDATE April 2026: The website gap has been closed. All four major press features, her complete story, the full menu, catering options, and social proof are now live on the website. The next opportunity is to move from a GitHub subdomain to a custom domain (asaalfurat.com) and to drive deeper integration with email list building and direct ordering to reduce third-party commission losses.

Cold Call / Walk-In Script
📞 3-Step Approach
1
The Opener — Lead with the story, not the sale
"I read the 417 Magazine article about you — honestly one of the most compelling business stories I've come across in Springfield. I specialize in helping local businesses like yours turn press coverage into customers. Can I ask — how many new people do you think found you after that article ran?"

Let her answer. Whatever number she says, respond: "Here's the thing — that article is still live and people are still reading it. But when they Google 'Asa Al-Furat' after reading it, your website doesn't tell your story. It's essentially a blank page. The article does more for you than your own site."
2
The Demo — Show it on your phone, live
Pull up asa-al-furat.square.site on your phone right now, before you walk in. Show her. Then pull up the 417 Magazine article next to it. "This article — which someone else wrote about you for free — does 10x more selling than your website. Let me show you what a site built around your story would look like."

Then pull up a comparable well-done bakery site as a visual reference. The contrast sells itself.
3
The Close — Frame it as capturing what already exists
"You don't need me to create anything from scratch. The story is already written. The reviews are already there. The press coverage is already there. I'm just going to build a place online where all of it lives together, so every new customer who finds you gets the full picture — not just a blank Square page.

You've done the hard part. Your website just hasn't caught up yet."

If budget is a concern: "We start with the site — one flat fee, you own it forever. The delivery platforms I can add in the same week. And the first month of new orders you get without paying DoorDash 30% commission covers most of it."
Alternate Pitch Angles (If Needed)
💸 DoorDash Commission Angle
"DoorDash takes 15–30% of every order. If you average $500/week in delivery, that's $75–150 going to DoorDash, not you. Square's own online ordering has zero per-order commission — customers order directly from your site and 100% of the revenue is yours. The site pays for itself in 3–6 months."
📱 Catering Angle
"Baklava trays for office events, weddings, Ramadan gatherings — downtown Springfield has law firms, medical offices, and event venues all within 3 blocks of you. A simple catering inquiry form on your site would capture those orders. Right now there's no way for anyone to even ask."
📸 Instagram → Website Angle
"Your Instagram has people who are already fans. But Instagram can shadowban you, change the algorithm, or go down tomorrow. Your website is the one place you own permanently. Every Instagram post should have a link that takes followers to your site — right now that link goes nowhere useful."
🗺️ Tourism Angle
"Downtown Springfield gets visitors who've never tried Iraqi food. If someone Googles 'unique food downtown Springfield' or 'Iraqi bakery near me' — right now you might not show up, or if you do, they land on an empty page. A proper site with SEO would capture those searches every single day."
