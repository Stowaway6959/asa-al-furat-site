Migration Checklist & Canonical Warning

RISK: Duplicate Content Penalty During Transition

While both sites are live, Google may split your ranking authority — hurting both. Fix this on day one of launch:

  • 301 Redirects: Any link to the old Square URL must redirect to the new Cloudflare domain. Transfers 100% of SEO value ("link juice") to the new site.
  • Canonical Tag: Add <link rel="canonical" href="https://yourdomain.com/" /> in the <head> of every page. Tells Google: "This is the one true URL."
  • Google Search Console: Submit the new sitemap.xml immediately after launch and request a re-crawl.

TIMELINE RECOMMENDATION

Don't run both sites for more than 14 days. Set a hard cutover date and add 301 redirects on day one of launch to avoid splitting ranking signals.
